What I learned in Introduction to Computing

History of computers

How computing grew from early calculating tools to today's phones and cloud services.

Hardware and software

The parts of a computer (CPU, memory, storage, input and output devices) and how software tells them what to do.

Number systems

How computers store everything as binary, and how to convert between binary, decimal and hexadecimal.

Operating systems

How an OS manages files, programs and devices so the computer stays usable.

Networks and the internet

How devices connect and share data, and what makes the internet work.

Safe and responsible use

Protecting accounts with strong passwords, spotting scams, and respecting others online.
